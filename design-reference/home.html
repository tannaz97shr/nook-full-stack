Three things we're stubborn about

01

Roasted eleven days ago, tops

We take one 12kg drop a week from a roaster four suburbs over. Whatever's left on Sunday goes home with staff.

02

The pastry case is baked here

Laminated dough on Tuesdays and Fridays, so the croissants are gone by eleven. We're not sorry, but we are aware.

03

No music loud enough to shout over

Wifi's on the receipt. Stay as long as you like — we'd rather a full room than a fast one.
