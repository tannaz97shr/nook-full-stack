Since 2019

We took over a laundromat and kept the tiles

Rosa spent nine years making coffee for other people's cafes. Dan spent eleven fixing espresso machines in the back of a van. The lease on 41 Ashgrove came up in the winter of 2019 and neither of us had a better idea.

The front room, about six o'clock in June.

The first year we got almost everything wrong

We opened with fourteen drinks on the board and a grinder that couldn't keep up past eight in the morning. By March we'd cut the menu in half, moved the pastry case to where the queue actually forms, and learned that people will wait four minutes for a good flat white and not one second for a bad one.

What stayed from the laundromat: the green floor tiles, the north window, and the habit of knowing regulars by their order rather than their name. Dan still fixes the machines himself. Rosa still tastes every batch at six-fifteen.

"A cafe is just a room where someone has decided to be careful about small things."

Rosa Feld · co-owner
